# ADR 6: Runtime i18n with signals

**Status:** accepted

**Decision.** A small `I18nService` holds the language as a signal and exposes a `t()` pipe. Switching language flips `dir` and `lang` on `<html>` without a reload.

**Why not `@angular/localize`.** It builds one bundle per locale and needs a reload to switch. A runtime switch is a better UX for two languages, at the cost of manual extraction.
